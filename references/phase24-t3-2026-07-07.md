## Live-vs-dormant (why smoke gates this tranche adequately)

- **Boot-live**: fs.c's iinit (NINODE lock loop at main() — its marker is in
  the goldens) and fsinit via forkret (readsb superblock read + FSMAGIC
  check + initlog log recovery, all through bread → blk-ramdisk). These run
  on every golden boot.
- **Dormant by architecture**: every sys_* body (BPF ecall dispatch owns the
  surface), pipe (pipe(4) unimplemented), fs walks (in-BPF fs_walk, ADR-078
  owns user FS reads). Kept verbatim at stock strength — they are the attack
  surface of the T4 trap-flip decision: sys_sbrk's overflow guards, argfd's
  bounds ladder, sys_exec's MAXARG cap + uarg==0 terminator, bmap's bounds
  panics, readi/writei's off/size overflow guards, namex's hand-over-hand
  locking.
