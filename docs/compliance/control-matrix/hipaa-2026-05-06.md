# HIPAA Security Rule + Privacy Rule + Breach Notification Control-Coverage Matrix

**Date:** 2026-05-06
**Author:** Marshal post-shift extension (NORTH-STAR Appendix A Phase E4)
**Reference:** 45 CFR Parts 160 & 164 (HIPAA Security/Privacy/Breach Rules), as updated by HITECH Act and Omnibus Rule
**Status legend:** **MAPPED** = full coverage with evidence | **PARTIAL** = some coverage, gap noted | **GAP** = no kingdom work yet | **N/A-DESIGN** = not applicable by Unheaded's architectural posture | **ADOPTER-OWNS** = Covered Entity (CE) responsibility | **DEFERRED** = planned, not shipped | **UNVERIFIED** = MoatGhost confirm

**Kingdom posture:** Unheaded is **never itself a Covered Entity** (CE). It is at most a **Business Associate** (BA) when an adopter CE deploys Unheaded into a regulated environment. By architectural design, Unheaded does not access, store, or transmit Protected Health Information (PHI). Most controls below are therefore either ADOPTER-OWNS (the CE's responsibility) or N/A-DESIGN (Unheaded architecturally cannot trigger them). The matrix flags **CONTRIBUTES** as a sub-class where Unheaded provides infrastructure that supports an adopter CE's HIPAA posture even when Unheaded itself is not in scope.

**Critical artifact missing:** there is no Unheaded BAA template. This is the highest-leverage HIPAA gap. Listed as a top-priority deliverable below.

## Strongest mapped areas (BA negotiating leverage)

§164.312 Technical safeguards — access control, audit controls, integrity, transmission security — is the kingdom's HIPAA bedrock. The BAA template should lead with the technical-safeguard story; the administrative-safeguard story has more gaps and benefits from the "we don't access PHI" architectural framing.

## Where the architectural framing wins

Like PCI, HIPAA is most easily cleared by **establishing scope**: Unheaded does not access, store, transmit, maintain, or process PHI. The BAA template should explicitly define this — making §164.502 (uses & disclosures) trivially compliant by virtue of no PHI ever being in scope. This positions Unheaded as a BA-with-architectural-isolation rather than a BA-with-PHI-access.
