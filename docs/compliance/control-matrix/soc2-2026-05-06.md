# SOC 2 (2017 TSC) Control-Coverage Matrix

**Date:** 2026-05-06
**Author:** Marshal post-shift extension (NORTH-STAR Appendix A Phase E2)
**Reference:** AICPA Trust Services Criteria (2017, 2022 Points of Focus update)
**Status legend:** **MAPPED** = full coverage with evidence | **PARTIAL** = some coverage, gap noted | **GAP** = no kingdom work yet | **N/A** = not applicable | **DEFERRED** = planned, not shipped | **UNVERIFIED** = MoatGhost confirm

**Scope assumption:** Type 1 readiness scoping. Type 2 requires sustained operating effectiveness across 6+ months — out of reach until consistent operations are documented and a single-developer kingdom acquires either staff or formally documented sole-operator controls (often acceptable to small auditors with caveat).

**Kingdom posture caveat:** SOC 2 leans heavily on **organizational** controls (personnel, governance, board oversight). A pre-funding single-operator kingdom passes **technical** criteria more easily than **organizational** ones. Many CC1, CC2, CC3, CC4 entries below are GAP for organizational reasons — this is normal at this stage and is the largest aggregate gap area.

## Headline gaps (priority order for MoatGhost)

1. **CC1 organizational controls** — single-operator kingdom; document the sole-operator posture explicitly (auditors generally accept this with rationale).
2. **CC7.4 / CC7.5 incident response + recovery runbooks** — major gap; reusable across SOC2, GDPR Art. 33, HIPAA §164.308(a)(6), PCI 12.10, NIST IR family.
3. **CC9.2 vendor risk management** — third-party (553 deps) assessment cadence missing.
4. **C1.2 data disposal policy** — single-page, high-leverage.
5. **A1.3 DR/recovery testing** — no chaos test, no formal RTO/RPO.
6. **CC3.1 / CC3.2 risk register** — enterprise-wide risk register missing; would consolidate threat models that are scattered today.

## Strongest mapped areas

CC5, CC6, CC7.1, CC7.2, CC8, PI1.1, PI1.5 — these are the kingdom's technical-control bedrock and would survive auditor inspection cleanly. SOC 2 readiness story should lead with these.

## Type 1 vs Type 2 readiness

- **Type 1** (point-in-time design): plausible 6-12 weeks if the headline gaps above close.
- **Type 2** (operating effectiveness over 6+ months): requires those gaps closed PLUS sustained operation evidence — ticket history, control execution evidence, periodic reviews. Realistically 12+ months out, even from a tight Type 1.
