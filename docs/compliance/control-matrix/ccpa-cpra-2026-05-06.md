# CCPA / CPRA Control-Coverage Matrix

**Date:** 2026-05-06
**Author:** Marshal post-shift extension (NORTH-STAR Appendix A Phase E15)
**Reference:** California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA — Cal. Civ. Code §§ 1798.100–1798.199.100) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA — Prop. 24, 2020) + CPPA (California Privacy Protection Agency) regulations (effective 2023+)
**Status legend:** **MAPPED** = full coverage with evidence | **PARTIAL** = some coverage, gap noted | **GAP** = no kingdom work yet | **N/A-DESIGN** = not applicable by Unheaded's architectural posture | **ADOPTER-OWNS** = adopter (business) responsibility | **DEFERRED** = planned, not shipped | **UNVERIFIED** = MoatGhost confirm

**Kingdom posture:** CCPA/CPRA imposes obligations on **businesses** that collect personal information (PI) about California consumers. Unheaded is **not itself a CCPA "business"** by architectural design — it does not collect, store, or process consumer PI. It would be a **service provider** (the CCPA equivalent of GDPR processor) when an adopter business deploys Unheaded into a CA-PI-handling environment. By design, Unheaded sees no PI either.

**Critical artifact missing:** there is no Unheaded service-provider / contractor agreement template (CCPA §1798.140(j) defines specific contract requirements). Same shape as the missing GDPR DPA (E1) and HIPAA BAA (E4).

**Threshold check:** CCPA applies to businesses meeting one of: (a) annual gross revenue > $25M, (b) buys/sells PI of ≥100,000 CA consumers/households/devices, (c) ≥50% revenue from selling/sharing PI. Unheaded does not meet any threshold today. **CCPA scope risk is via adopters, not direct.**

**CPRA additions over CCPA:** Right to Correct, Right to Limit (sensitive PI), Sensitive Personal Information category, Service Provider vs Contractor distinction, CPPA establishment, data-minimization principle, opt-out preference signals (Global Privacy Control).

## Sensitive Personal Information (SPI) — CPRA §1798.140(ae)

CPRA defines SPI categories that trigger Right to Limit (§1798.121):

- Government identifiers (SSN, driver's license, etc.)
- Account login + credentials
- Precise geolocation
- Racial / ethnic origin / religious belief / union membership
- Genetic data
- Biometric data for unique identification
- Health information
- Sex life / sexual orientation
- Mail, email, or text content not directed to the business

**Kingdom posture:** Unheaded does not architecturally process any of these. **N/A-DESIGN** for the entire SPI category — same architectural floor as the broader CCPA scope.

## Service Provider vs Contractor (CPRA distinction)

CPRA §1798.140(ai) introduces "Contractor" as a subcategory distinct from Service Provider:

- **Service Provider** — processes PI on behalf of the business pursuant to a written contract.
- **Contractor** — receives PI from the business pursuant to a written contract for a business purpose.

The distinction matters for some downstream obligations. **Status:** the universal contract-template gap applies to both.

## Multi-State Privacy Law Crosswalk (informational)

CCPA/CPRA was the first comprehensive U.S. state privacy law. Other states (Virginia VCDPA, Colorado CPA, Connecticut CTDPA, Utah UCPA, Texas TDPSA, Iowa, Indiana, Tennessee, Montana, Oregon, Delaware, NH, NJ, etc.) have followed with similar frameworks. The kingdom's CCPA matrix maps closely to all of them — **same gaps, same architectural floor.**

If the kingdom adds a single privacy-notice + DSR mechanism + service-provider template, it covers ~80% of all U.S. state privacy laws simultaneously. **High leverage.**

## Headline gaps

1. **Service-provider / contractor agreement template** (universal — closes CCPA + GDPR + HIPAA simultaneously).
2. **Public privacy notice** (closes CCPA §1798.130 + GDPR Art. 12 + NIST PT-5).
3. **Storage / retention policy** (closes CPRA §1798.140(o) + GDPR Art. 5(1)(e) + PCI 10.5 + HIPAA §164.316 + NIST AU-11).
4. **Breach response runbook** (CCPA §1798.150 private right of action + GDPR Art. 33 + HIPAA §164.410 + universal IR gap).

## What architecturally wins

Like GDPR, the kingdom's strongest CCPA story is **scope-out**: Unheaded does not collect, store, or process PI. Document that in a service-provider template + privacy notice and CCPA scope falls away to the adopter business.
