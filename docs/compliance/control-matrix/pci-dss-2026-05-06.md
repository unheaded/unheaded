# PCI DSS v4.0 Control-Coverage Matrix

**Date:** 2026-05-06
**Author:** Marshal post-shift extension (NORTH-STAR Appendix A Phase E3)
**Reference:** PCI DSS v4.0 (March 2022, latest published baseline)
**Status legend:** **MAPPED** = full coverage with evidence | **PARTIAL** = some coverage, gap noted | **GAP** = no kingdom work yet | **N/A-DESIGN** = not applicable by Unheaded's architectural posture | **ADOPTER-OWNS** = adopter responsibility | **DEFERRED** = planned, not shipped | **UNVERIFIED** = MoatGhost confirm

**Critical scope assumption:** Unheaded is **never itself in PCI scope** by design. Unheaded does not store, process, or transmit cardholder data (CHD) or sensitive authentication data (SAD). Most controls below are therefore either:

- **N/A-DESIGN** — does not apply because Unheaded architecturally cannot touch CHD/SAD.
- **ADOPTER-OWNS** — applies to the adopter's deployment of their own application stack on top of Unheaded.
- **CONTRIBUTES** — Unheaded does provide controls that *support* the adopter's PCI compliance even though Unheaded itself isn't a CDE component.

The matrix below uses CONTRIBUTES as a **status sub-class** when the kingdom provides infrastructure-level controls that an adopter could lean on for their PCI scope.

## Headline gaps (priority order for adopter PCI scoping)

1. **12.10 Incident response plan** — universal gap across this matrix, GDPR Art. 33, SOC 2 CC7.4, HIPAA §164.308(a)(6), NIST IR family, FedRAMP IR family. Single document closes 6 controls across 6 frameworks.
2. **10.5 Audit log retention policy** — single-page document.

4. **2.2.1 / F4** — auto-disable SA token mounting on every helm Deployment that doesn't call kube-apiserver.
5. **11.3 Third-party pen-test of record** — adopter needs this; BlackMage daily loop is internal validation.

## Strongest mapped areas (adopter can lean on these)

Req 1 (NSC), Req 4 (transmission cryptography), Req 6 (secure SDLC), Req 10 (logging/monitoring), Req 11.4-11.5 (IDS + FIM) — these are where the kingdom's substrate genuinely contributes to an adopter's PCI scope. Document these as the "Unheaded contribution" page in any adopter's PCI ROC.

## What MUST stay scoped OUT of Unheaded

Req 3 (stored CHD) and Req 9 (physical) are deliberately scoped out. The architectural promise — *Unheaded never sees CHD/SAD* — is the moat that keeps Unheaded itself out of an adopter's CDE. The day Unheaded ingests CHD, this matrix ceases to be valid and a much heavier audit kicks in.
