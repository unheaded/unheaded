How to read this. Each Unheaded common control is one engineering practice, evidenced by checks that can fail (CI jobs, gate scripts, commit signatures), and mapped onto requirements in many frameworks at once. A requirement is EVIDENCED only when every control mapped to it has current passing evidence; that means the evidence supports it, not that an assessor would find it satisfied. FAILING: a mapped control fails. INCOMPLETE: mapped, but evidence is missing or stale. UNMAPPED: no control yet; still counted. FedRAMP baselines take their mappings from 800-53. Details: ADR-097.

Open findings

The count comes from evidence; triage from compliance/findings/register.yaml. A finding closes only when its check passes. Step is the remediation order (ADR-098): safe first, lockout risk last.
