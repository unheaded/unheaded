# Unheaded Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Unheaded is committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all community members, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Insulting or derogatory comments
- Personal attacks
- Publishing private information without consent
- Any other conduct that violates applicable laws

## Our Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they consider inappropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community interactions:
- GitHub repositories and issues
- Pull requests and code reviews
- Discussion forums
- Email communications

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior should be reported by contacting stevie@bellis.tech.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated fairly. The identity of the reporter will be protected.
