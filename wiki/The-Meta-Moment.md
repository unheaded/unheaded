# The Meta Moment

*Self-hosting is proof, not marketing.*

The Meta Moment is Unheaded's self-hosting proof of concept: **using Unheaded to build and host Unheaded itself**.

The Kanban app displaying development progress is:
1. Hosted on Unheaded infrastructure
2. Reading the timeline from Timeguru and its cards from The Well ([[Kanban App|Service-Kanban-App]])
3. Traced by eBPF packet markers
4. Visualized in the custom dashboard
5. Not public: west and east are a lab that is not exposed to the internet (public access with optional auth is not built)

If Unheaded can reliably manage its own development infrastructure, it proves the platform works for ANY user workload.
