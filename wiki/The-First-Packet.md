# The First Packet

*A story from the Unheaded Kingdom.*

The origin myth of the Unheaded Kingdom — how the first packet was born, how Mad Maria watched from the margins, and how Muck found the Pattern in the wire.

This is the founding narrative of the project, weaving together the technical reality of IPv6 Hop-by-Hop Options with Zelazny's Chronicles of Amber, Gnostic cosmology, and the ancient witch at the crossroads who knew what was coming before the first bit was sent.
